# FCGS Student & Parent Portal - User Manual

---

## PART 1: STUDENT PORTAL

Welcome to the FCGS Student Portal. This guide will walk you through every feature available to you as a student.

---

### 1.1 Logging In

1. Open the FCGS system in your web browser.
2. On the login screen, select **Student** as your login type.
3. Choose your **School** from the dropdown list.
4. Enter your **Username** and **Password** (provided by your school administrator).
5. Click **Sign In**.

If you have trouble logging in, contact your school administrator to reset your password or check your account status.

---

### 1.2 Dashboard

After logging in, you land on your **Dashboard** -- your home screen. From here, you can access all sections of the portal using the menu on the left side of the screen.

---

### 1.3 My Classes

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Learning > My Classes

This page shows:

- **Your Assigned Class** -- The class/form you are enrolled in (e.g. "Form 2A", "Grade 5 Blue").
- **Your Subjects** -- A table listing every subject you are taking, including:
  - Subject name
  - Syllabus code (e.g. ZIMSEC or Cambridge code, if applicable)
  - The teacher assigned to that subject

If you are not yet assigned to a class, a message will appear asking you to contact your school administrator.

---

### 1.4 My Grades

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Assessments > My Grades

This page shows all your assessment results. At the top, you will see three summary cards:

- **Total Assessments** -- How many tests/exams have been recorded.
- **Overall Average** -- Your average percentage across all assessments.
- **Subjects** -- How many subjects you have grades for.

**Filtering your grades:**

- Use the **Term** dropdown to view grades for a specific academic term (e.g. "2025 - Term 1").
- Use the **Subject** dropdown to view grades for one particular subject.

**The grades table** shows each assessment with:
- Subject name and code
- Assessment name (e.g. "Mid-Term Test", "End of Year Exam")
- Assessment type (e.g. "Test", "Exam")
- Your percentage score
- Your grade (colour-coded: green for top grades like A/A*, neutral for mid-range, red for lower grades)
- Teacher's comment

---

### 1.5 My Attendance

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Records > My Attendance

This page tracks your daily attendance. At the top, you see five summary cards:

- **Attendance Rate** -- Your overall attendance as a percentage.
- **Total Days** -- Number of school days recorded.
- **Present** -- Days you were marked present.
- **Absent** -- Days you were marked absent.
- **Late** -- Days you arrived late.

**Filtering:**

- Use the **Month** dropdown to view attendance for a specific month.

**The attendance table** shows each day with:
- Date (with day of the week)
- Status (colour-coded badge: green for Present, red for Absent, amber for Late)
- Reason (if one was recorded, e.g. "Sick leave", "Family emergency")

---

### 1.6 Newsletters

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Community > Newsletters

This page shows all published school newsletters and announcements that are relevant to students. Each newsletter appears as a card showing:

- Newsletter title
- Audience tag (e.g. "Students", "All")
- Date published

**Reading a newsletter:** Click on any newsletter card to expand it and read the full content. Click it again to collapse it.

---

### 1.7 My Profile

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Account > My Profile

This is a read-only page showing your personal information on file. It includes:

**Personal Information:**
- Your full name and avatar (initials)
- Your role badge ("Student")
- Your admission number
- Your account status (Active/Inactive)
- Email address (if on file)
- Phone number (if on file)
- Home address
- Gender
- Date of birth

**Academic Information:**
- Your current class
- Enrollment date
- Parent/Guardian name
- Boarding status (if applicable)

If any information is incorrect, please ask your school administrator or parent to update it.

---

### 1.8 Notifications

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Account > Notifications

This page shows all messages and alerts sent to you by the school. Each notification displays:

- A channel icon showing how it was sent (Email, SMS, or Newsletter)
- The notification title
- The message content
- The date and time it was sent
- A "New" badge if you haven't read it yet

**Actions you can take:**
- **Click on a notification** to mark it as read.
- **Mark All Read** button (top right) -- marks every unread notification as read at once.

## PART 2: PARENT PORTAL

Welcome to the FCGS Parent Portal. This guide covers every feature available to you as a parent or guardian.

---

### 2.1 Logging In

1. Open the FCGS system in your web browser.
2. On the login screen, select **Parent** as your login type.
3. Choose your **School** from the dropdown list.
4. Enter your **Username** and **Password** (provided by your school administrator).
5. Click **Sign In**.

If you have trouble logging in, contact your school administrator.

---

### 2.2 Dashboard

After logging in, you land on your **Dashboard**. Use the menu on the left side to navigate to all available sections.

---

### 2.3 Children's Attendance

**Where to find it:** Left menu > My Children > Attendance

This page lets you monitor your children's daily school attendance.

**If you have more than one child at the school:**
- Tabs with each child's name appear at the top. Click a tab to switch between children.

**For each child, you will see:**

- A card showing the child's name, initials, and admission number.
- **Five summary cards:**
  - Attendance Rate (percentage)
  - Total Days recorded
  - Present days
  - Absent days
  - Late days

**Filtering:**
- Use the **Month** dropdown to narrow results to a specific month.

**The attendance table** shows each recorded day with:
- Date (with day of the week)
- Status (colour-coded: green = Present, red = Absent, amber = Late)
- Reason (if provided)

If no children are linked to your account, a message will appear -- contact your school administrator to link your children.

---

### 2.4 Results & Reports

**Where to find it:** Left menu > My Children > Results & Reports

This page shows your children's academic grades and assessment results.

**If you have more than one child:**
- Tabs with each child's name appear at the top. Click a tab to switch between children.

**For each child, you will see:**

- A card showing the child's name and admission number.
- **Three summary cards:**
  - Total Assessments -- number of tests/exams on record
  - Overall Average -- average percentage
  - Subjects -- number of subjects with grades

**Filtering:**
- Use the **Term** dropdown to see results from a specific term.
- Use the **Subject** dropdown to see results for one subject only.

**The grades table** shows:
- Subject name and code
- Assessment name
- Assessment type
- Percentage score
- Grade (colour-coded)
- Teacher's comment

---

### 2.5 Newsletters

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Communication > Newsletters

This page displays all published school newsletters and announcements directed at parents (or the whole school community). Each newsletter shows:

- Title
- Audience tag (e.g. "Parents", "All")
- Date published

**Reading a newsletter:** Click on any newsletter card to expand and read the full content. Click again to collapse.

---

### 2.6 My Profile

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Account > My Profile

A read-only page showing your personal information on file:

**Personal Information:**
- Your full name and initials avatar
- Your role badge ("Parent")
- Email address
- Phone number
- Home address

**Additional Information:**
- Occupation
- Relationship to student(s) (e.g. Father, Mother, Guardian)

If anything is incorrect, contact your school administrator to update your details.

---

### 2.7 Notifications

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Account > Notifications

Shows all messages and alerts sent to you by the school. Each notification shows:

- Channel icon (Email, SMS, or Newsletter)
- Title and message
- Date and time
- "New" badge for unread notifications

**Actions you can take:**
- **Click a notification** to mark it as read.
- **Mark All Read** button -- marks all unread notifications as read at once.

---

### 2.8 Children's Profiles

**Where to find it:** Left menu > My Children > Profiles

View basic profile information for each of your children enrolled at the school.

---

### 2.9 Library

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Learning Support > Library

Access the school's library system to browse the book catalog, check availability, and view digital resources (when the Library module is enabled by the school).

---

### 2.10 Fees & Payments

**Where to find it:** Left menu > Finance > Fees & Payments

View your children's fee balances and payment history (when the Finance module is enabled by the school).

## TIPS & TROUBLESHOOTING

- **Can't see your data?** Make sure your school administrator has properly linked your account. Students need to be assigned to a class, and parents need their children linked to their account.
- **Forgot your password?** Contact your school administrator to reset it.
- **Information looks wrong?** Profile information is managed by school staff. Ask them to correct any errors.
- **Notifications not showing?** Notifications appear when the school sends you a message via email, SMS, or newsletter. Check back later if you don't see any yet.
- **Some menu items say "Coming Soon"?** These features are still being developed and will become available in future updates.
